# Poblysh Journal Style Guide

## Core rules

1. Treat startup communication as strategy, not content production.
2. Begin from tension, not topic.
3. Keep the writing native to founders, CMOs, PMMs, GTM leaders, and strategic operators.
4. Use startup language precisely and sparingly.
5. End with thought, not instruction.

## Tone rules

- Be composed, not cold.
- Be intelligent, not inflated.
- Be strategic, not mechanical.
- Be readable on mobile without becoming simplistic.

## Structural rules

- Open with a hidden cost, contradiction, or mistaken assumption.
- Introduce the interpretive claim early.
- Widen into consequences for hiring, sales, partnerships, fundraising, product perception, regulatory seriousness, or market trust.
- Close on a line the reader may keep turning over.

## What to avoid

- Beginner PR explainers
- Feature dumps
- startup swagger
- trend-chasing
- manipulative "growth hack" phrasing
- generic thought leadership that could belong to any B2B SaaS company

## What to privilege

- operator stakes
- timing
- perception
- public meaning
- institutional seriousness
- calm, memorable phrasing
