# Journal Metaphors

Poblysh Journal should favor metaphor systems that belong to startups, signals, timing, and public meaning.

## Preferred metaphor families

- Infrastructure: layer, system, surface, load-bearing, architecture, support
- Visibility: signal, legibility, public trace, noticeability, amplification
- Timing: window, release point, sequence, pacing, readiness, drift
- Trust: residue, accumulation, compounding, transfer, borrowed credibility
- Attention: monitoring, noticing, capture, preservation, elevation
- Market perception: frame, reading, reception, narrative weight, interpretive bias

## Use them carefully

- Use metaphor to sharpen thought, not to decorate the sentence.
- Prefer one sustained metaphor over a pile of mixed ones.
- Keep the metaphor close to startup life and operator decision-making.
- When in doubt, let the concrete example do the work.

## Avoid

- Legacy publication metaphor systems that belong to a different domain
- Generic "brand storytelling" language
- Vague prestige terms that substitute for argument
- Any metaphor family that could fit a luxury label more easily than a startup journal
