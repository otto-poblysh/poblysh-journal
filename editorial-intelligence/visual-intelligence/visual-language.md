# Poblysh Journal Visual Language

## Purpose

This document defines the visual system for Poblysh Journal's mobile-first latazin essays.

The journal should feel like a calm editorial object for serious startup readers, not a shrunk desktop blog, a social carousel, or a SaaS brand deck.

## Core thesis

Poblysh Journal should hold:
- institutional seriousness
- mobile intimacy

The resulting feeling is:

**quiet authority with editorial pulse**

## Governing design principles

1. Rhythm before decoration
2. Breath is part of the brand
3. Restraint creates distinction
4. Typography carries thought; imagery carries temperature
5. Repetition builds comfort

## Format foundation

- primary format: mobile-first latazin
- aspect ratio: 5:8
- grid: 3 columns by 4 rows
- reading context: one-handed, interrupted, transit-friendly, pause-friendly

## Color strategy

Stay inside a monochrome editorial spectrum:
- paper white
- bone
- soft ash
- light slate
- iron
- charcoal
- near-black
- true black

Use true black sparingly for threshold, divider, and residue pages.

## Typography strategy

Use:
- Merriweather for titles, phrase pages, section turns, quiet pull quotes
- Lato for body text, captions, labels, metadata

Merriweather is the voice of reflection and gravity.
Lato is the voice of readable flow and clarity.

## Page archetypes

### Cover / Threshold
- one title
- strong negative space
- sets tone without summarizing

### Phrase Page
- one line
- large spatial calm
- resets the eye

### Reading Page
- up to five sentences
- stable margins
- no unnecessary experimentation

### Image-Only Page
- one atmospheric or symbolic image
- no explanatory burden

### Bridge Page
- one image plus one short line
- introduces the next conceptual movement

### Closing Residue Page
- one sentence or short phrase
- no CTA
- leaves a thought behind

## Image philosophy

Imagery should not behave like literal illustration. It should deepen the emotional weather of the essay.

Favor:
- quiet spaces
- architecture and thresholds
- work objects treated symbolically
- reflective glass, tables, notebooks, folders, windows
- human editorial fragments such as posture, silhouette, hands, or profile

Avoid:
- startup stock clichés
- laptops-as-meaning
- handshakes
- smiling office teams
- dashboards used literally
- anything that feels like ad creative

## Image mood

Images should generally feel:
- monochrome or near-monochrome
- low-saturation when color appears
- tactile
- directional in light
- quiet but alert
- serious without becoming gloomy

## Layout behavior

Use the 3 x 4 grid as quiet compositional discipline.

- body pages should usually sit inside two columns or the optical equivalent
- rows should control top, middle, and bottom placement
- negative space should be used to slow reading and separate ideas

## Signature devices

- threshold phrase
- hinge page
- quiet pull quote
- monochrome object page
- closing residue page

## What the journal must not look like

Do not let Poblysh resemble:
- a startup carousel built for engagement hacks
- a SaaS pitch deck in swipe form
- a productivity post series
- a loud thought-leadership PDF

## Success test

The system is working when the reader feels:
- calm enough to continue
- curious enough to turn the next page
- respected rather than manipulated
- that Poblysh has taste without trying too hard to prove it
