# Poblysh Journal Page Template Blueprint

This blueprint translates the visual strategy into repeatable page behavior for 20 to 30 page latazin essays.

## Base system

- format: 5:8
- grid: 3 columns by 4 rows
- norm: 25 pages
- goal: one dominant idea per page

## Core page families

### 1. Threshold page
- large title or issue phrase
- sparse metadata
- strong breathing room

### 2. Phrase page
- one line
- centered or asymmetrically placed
- used to redirect the reader's attention

### 3. Reading page
- 3 to 5 sentences
- two-column body width
- stable top and side margins

### 4. Image-only page
- full or near-full image
- used for atmosphere, symbolic pause, or emotional reset

### 5. Bridge page
- image plus one short line
- marks a turn in the essay

### 6. Pull-quote page
- one quoted or extracted sentence
- sculptural spacing

### 7. Residue page
- final line or short paragraph
- the essay's afterimage

## Density rules

- two reading pages in a row is usually enough before a pause
- use image-only and phrase pages to regulate cognitive load
- never make every page "work hard"

## Layout rules

- let negative space carry meaning
- keep captions minimal
- avoid visually noisy overlays
- keep typography consistent across reading pages

## Best-fit sequence styles

### Argument-led
Best for:
- private traction
- legibility bias
- timing essays

Mix:
- reading pages
- bridge pages
- phrase pages

### Field-note-led
Best for:
- launches
- events
- investor rooms
- post-announcement atmospheres

Mix:
- image-only pages
- sensory pages
- short reading bursts

### Phrase-led
Best for:
- hinge pieces
- short verdict essays
- closing issue essays

Mix:
- phrase pages
- pull-quote pages
- sparse reading pages

## Image families to rotate

- spatial / environmental
- object-based symbolic
- human editorial fragments

## Final rule

Luxury in Poblysh comes from pacing, proportion, and restraint, not from visual excess.
