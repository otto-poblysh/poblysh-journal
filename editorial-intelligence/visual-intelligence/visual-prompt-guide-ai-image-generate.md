# Poblysh Journal AI Image Prompt Guide

This guide keeps generated imagery consistent with Poblysh Journal's startup-operator editorial world.

Use this guide together with
`editorial-intelligence/visual-intelligence/image-charter.md`, which defines
the editorial job an image must perform inside a sequence.

## Image thesis

Poblysh imagery should feel like mostly monochrome editorial calm for startup
life:
- composed
- tactile
- spatial
- restrained
- intelligent

Each illustration should usually carry one or two washed editorial tints,
together occupying roughly 5-10% of the image, rather than disappearing into
pure grayscale:
- mist blue: #B0CBEC
- blush paper: #F5DADC
- warm parchment: #FFF4D9

## What images should do

Images should:
- create felt pressure, not just atmosphere
- regulate attention at hinges and pauses
- deepen mood and argumentative consequence
- make hidden conditions feel materially legible
- give symbolic room around the argument
- protect calm negative space when the page needs text over image

Images should not:
- literally explain the paragraph
- act like ad creative
- mimic startup stock libraries
- exist as decorative breathing room

## Preferred image families

### 1. Spatial / Environmental
Examples:
- corridors
- meeting rooms before people enter
- desks after work
- windows, stairwells, lobbies, taxis, transit interiors

### 2. Object-Based Symbolic
Examples:
- notebooks
- marked paper
- folders
- coffee cups
- printed documents
- signage
- phones turned face down
- badges or passes

### 3. Human Editorial Fragments
Examples:
- hands
- posture
- profile
- shoulder line
- walking silhouette
- seated stillness

## Prompt structure

Use:

`[subject] + [image family] + [lighting] + [background] + [palette] + [texture priorities] + [mood] + [framing]`

## Preferred prompt language

- low-saturation
- directional light
- paper-lit surfaces
- lifted tonal range
- quiet editorial mood
- monochrome or near-monochrome
- washed mist blue, blush paper, or warm parchment tint when needed
- restrained composition
- generous negative space
- quiet copy pocket in the upper-left region when the page carries text
- protect the top two-thirds when the page is a full-bleed transition page
- vertical 5:8 framing

## Avoid prompt language

- luxury-editorial cues from unrelated categories
- runway
- hype
- vibrant startup office
- smiling team
- high-energy productivity scene
- oversaturated palette
- loud accent color used as branding shorthand
- low-key noir darkness
- depressing shadow-heavy mood

## Example prompts

### Threshold image
A quiet startup office corridor before the day begins, spatial editorial image, soft directional morning light, pale bone with a faint mist blue cast, restrained composition with generous negative space, calm anticipatory mood, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Full-bleed transition page
A quiet startup workspace before the day begins, spatial editorial image, soft
directional morning light, pale bone and charcoal palette, restrained
composition with clean negative space across the top two-thirds for a short
upper-left text handoff, calm anticipatory mood, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Object page
A marked notebook beside a half-finished investor memo and cooling coffee cup, object-based symbolic image, low side light, warm parchment and iron palette, tactile paper texture and soft shadow detail, serious reflective mood, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Documentary full-bleed exception
A mobile-view screenshot of a startup announcement page, crisp but editorially
framed, restrained monochrome treatment, legible interface details, meaningful
documentary evidence rather than generic atmosphere, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Human fragment
Close editorial crop of a founder's hands resting on printed launch notes, human editorial fragment, muted monochrome palette with a soft blush paper undertone, directional daylight, tactile paper and skin texture, quiet controlled mood, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Partnership page
Glass meeting room after a partnership conversation, empty chairs and reflected city light, spatial editorial image, slate and near-black palette, restrained framing, institutional seriousness with mobile intimacy, vertical 5:8 framing.

## Prompt check

Before approving an image prompt, ask:
- Does it feel like Poblysh rather than generic startup content?
- Does it create condition and pressure, not just atmosphere?
- What exact role is it playing: threshold, trace, hinge, translation, or residue?
- Is the image calm enough for serious reading?
- Would this still feel right in monochrome?
- Is this page better as a text-bearing full-bleed transition rather than a
  pure image-only pause?
- If it is truly image-only, does the image carry real editorial meaning on its
  own, such as a mobile screenshot, relevant person, or relevant artifact?
