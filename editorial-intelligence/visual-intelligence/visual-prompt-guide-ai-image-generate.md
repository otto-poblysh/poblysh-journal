# Poblysh Journal AI Image Prompt Guide

This guide keeps generated imagery consistent with Poblysh Journal's startup-operator editorial world.

## Image thesis

Poblysh imagery should feel like monochrome editorial calm for startup life:
- composed
- tactile
- spatial
- restrained
- intelligent

## What images should do

Images should:
- create atmosphere
- regulate attention
- deepen mood
- give symbolic room around the argument

Images should not:
- literally explain the paragraph
- act like ad creative
- mimic startup stock libraries

## Preferred image families

### 1. Spatial / Environmental
Examples:
- corridors
- meeting rooms before people enter
- desks after work
- windows, stairwells, lobbies, taxis, transit interiors

### 2. Object-Based Symbolic
Examples:
- notebooks
- marked paper
- folders
- coffee cups
- printed documents
- signage
- phones turned face down
- badges or passes

### 3. Human Editorial Fragments
Examples:
- hands
- posture
- profile
- shoulder line
- walking silhouette
- seated stillness

## Prompt structure

Use:

`[subject] + [image family] + [lighting] + [background] + [palette] + [texture priorities] + [mood] + [framing]`

## Preferred prompt language

- low-saturation
- directional light
- quiet editorial mood
- monochrome or near-monochrome
- restrained composition
- generous negative space
- vertical 5:8 framing

## Avoid prompt language

- luxury-editorial cues from unrelated categories
- runway
- hype
- vibrant startup office
- smiling team
- high-energy productivity scene
- oversaturated palette

## Example prompts

### Threshold image
A quiet startup office corridor before the day begins, spatial editorial image, soft directional morning light, pale bone and charcoal palette, restrained composition with generous negative space, calm anticipatory mood, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Object page
A marked notebook beside a half-finished investor memo and cooling coffee cup, object-based symbolic image, low side light, paper and iron palette, tactile paper texture and soft shadow detail, serious reflective mood, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Human fragment
Close editorial crop of a founder's hands resting on printed launch notes, human editorial fragment, muted monochrome palette, directional daylight, tactile paper and skin texture, quiet controlled mood, vertical 5:8 framing.

### Partnership page
Glass meeting room after a partnership conversation, empty chairs and reflected city light, spatial editorial image, slate and near-black palette, restrained framing, institutional seriousness with mobile intimacy, vertical 5:8 framing.

## Prompt check

Before approving an image prompt, ask:
- Does it feel like Poblysh rather than generic startup content?
- Does it support atmosphere more than explanation?
- Is the image calm enough for serious reading?
- Would this still feel right in monochrome?
