## Page Strategy Summary

This sequence treats the essay as editorial raw material rather than fixed page
copy. The article's central argument is that startup progress can be real,
substantial, and commercially important while still failing to compound because
it never becomes public meaning. That means the sequence should not begin with
explanation. It should begin with absence, distance, and a feeling that motion
is happening somewhere just outside the visible frame.

The page structure therefore moves in five stages: **absence**, **naming the
wound**, **business consequence**, **visibility debt**, and **systems
resolution**. The visual rhythm alternates between full-bleed transition pages,
statement pages, object studies, and calm reading pages so the issue feels like
a mobile-native editorial object rather than a broken article.

Essay pages carry two to three paragraphs each so the reading experience feels
like an actual essay rather than an Instagram carousel. Single-paragraph essay
pages are not permitted unless the paragraph is unusually long or the page also
carries a subheading introduction.

Where a page uses full bleed for atmosphere rather than documentary proof, it
also carries a short transition line in the upper-left. True text-free full
bleed is reserved for images that already matter on their own, such as mobile
screenshots of announcements or interfaces, relevant people, or relevant
artifacts.

## Extraction and Editing Notes

* "A startup can be moving quickly and still become strategically invisible."
  should appear early as the first real thesis line.
* "Progress that never becomes public meaning often fails to compound into
  trust." should become a statement page.
* "Silence is not neutral." should stand alone.
* "What many companies carry is not merely a lack of awareness, but visibility
  debt." should anchor the late-middle section.
* "Traction that never becomes public meaning does not stay private. It leaks
  out later as friction." should become the final movement's hinge.
* Add light subheads: **The Cold Surface**, **What The Market Can Read**,
  **Visibility Debt**, **After Friction**.

## Closing Note

This sequence works because it does not flatten the essay into equal reading
blocks. It gives the article absence, threshold, business consequence, media
logic, visibility debt, and a systems turn before the final residue line. Essay
pages carry two to three paragraphs each so the reading experience feels like
a genuine essay rather than a swiped carousel. The full-bleed pages are no
longer cosmetic pauses; each one now carries a short upper-left handoff into
the next movement, while true text-free full bleed is kept in reserve for
documentary images that would genuinely earn it. The result preserves the
argument while making it feel genuinely Latazin-native and recognizably Poblysh.
