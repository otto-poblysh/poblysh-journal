# Create Latazin Pages Plan Prompt

Use this prompt to create a page-by-page sequence plan for a Poblysh Journal essay.

## Inputs to review first

1. The target essay
2. `editorial-intelligence/house-voice.md`
3. `editorial-intelligence/house-metaphors.md`
4. `editorial-intelligence/style-guide.md`
5. `editorial-intelligence/visual-intelligence/visual-language.md`
6. `editorial-intelligence/visual-intelligence/page-template-blueprint.md`
7. `editorial-intelligence/visual-intelligence/visual-prompt-guide-ai-image-generate.md`

## Planning objective

Turn the essay into a mobile-first 20 to 32 page latazin sequence with 25 pages
as the default.

Page count is editorial, not mechanical. Preserve the essay's full argument and
major turns first, then choose the length that lets the piece breathe without
cutting what matters.

The plan must feel:
- calm
- strategically paced
- breathable
- visually coherent with Poblysh

## Required outputs

### 1. Sequence summary
- essay title
- dominant tension
- dominant mood
- recommended length based on argument integrity, not a rigid page target
- whether the sequence is argument-led, field-note-led, phrase-led, or mixed

### 2. Page-by-page plan

For each page include:
- page number
- page family
- purpose
- text payload
- image direction if needed
- note on pacing or transition

### 3. Visual logic

Explain:
- where the breathing pages sit
- where the argument peaks
- where the reader gets reset
- how the final residue is protected

## Constraints

- do not over-pack reading pages
- do not explain the whole essay on the cover
- always give the cover page a Threshold image with generous negative space in
  the upper portion so the title and subtitle can overlay cleanly; covers must
  never be text-only on a flat color ground
- never cut core claims, evidence, examples, definitions, or hinge transitions
  merely to force the essay into a lower page count
- if the essay is long or multi-turn, extend the sequence before trimming;
  remove only repetition, throat-clearing, or sentence-level slack
- use phrase pages as rhythm tools, not filler
- essay pages must carry two to three paragraphs each so the reading experience
  feels like a genuine essay, not an Instagram carousel; a single-paragraph
  essay page is only acceptable when it also carries a subheading introduction
  or the paragraph itself is unusually long and structurally dense
- default image-led pause pages to **full-bleed transition pages** with one
  short upper-left line that hands the reader into the next essay page
- require the image on a text-bearing full-bleed page to protect clean negative
  space across roughly the top two-thirds so the text can sit quietly and stay
  legible
- reserve true text-free full-bleed pages for images that carry clear meaning
  on their own, especially mobile screenshots of announcements or interfaces,
  relevant people, and relevant artifacts or documents
- cosmetic atmosphere alone is not enough reason for a pure image-only page
- keep the overall sequence compatible with one-handed mobile reading

## Default length guidance

- 20 pages only for tightly argued short essays after confirming that nothing
  important has been cut
- 25 pages as the standard
- 27 to 30 pages when the essay needs more spatial pacing, fuller reading room,
  or more than one major argumentative turn
- 31 to 32 pages when that is the cleanest way to preserve the essay's full
  substance without crowding the mobile reading experience
