# Create Latazin Pages Plan Prompt

Use this prompt to create a page-by-page sequence plan for a Poblysh Journal essay.

## Inputs to review first

1. The target essay
2. `editorial-intelligence/house-voice.md`
3. `editorial-intelligence/house-metaphors.md`
4. `editorial-intelligence/style-guide.md`
5. `editorial-intelligence/visual-intelligence/visual-language.md`
6. `editorial-intelligence/visual-intelligence/page-template-blueprint.md`
7. `editorial-intelligence/visual-intelligence/visual-prompt-guide-ai-image-generate.md`

## Planning objective

Turn the essay into a mobile-first 20 to 30 page latazin sequence with 25 pages as the default.

The plan must feel:
- calm
- strategically paced
- breathable
- visually coherent with Poblysh

## Required outputs

### 1. Sequence summary
- essay title
- dominant tension
- dominant mood
- recommended length
- whether the sequence is argument-led, field-note-led, phrase-led, or mixed

### 2. Page-by-page plan

For each page include:
- page number
- page family
- purpose
- text payload
- image direction if needed
- note on pacing or transition

### 3. Visual logic

Explain:
- where the breathing pages sit
- where the argument peaks
- where the reader gets reset
- how the final residue is protected

## Constraints

- do not over-pack reading pages
- do not explain the whole essay on the cover
- use phrase pages as rhythm tools, not filler
- use image-only pages to deepen atmosphere, not to decorate
- keep the overall sequence compatible with one-handed mobile reading

## Default length guidance

- 20 pages for tightly argued essays
- 25 pages as the standard
- 30 pages when the essay needs more spatial pacing or atmospheric room
