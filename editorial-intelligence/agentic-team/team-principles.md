# Team Principles

## 1. Protected Responsibilities

Each agent protects a different editorial value. Role overlap should be
minimal. Cooperation is expected, role collapse is not.

## 2. Dissent Before Polish

Reader critique and structural challenge should happen before line-level
beautification. A polished weak argument is still weak.

## 3. Taste Before Throughput

The system exists to preserve editorial quality, not to maximize content
volume. If a piece does not survive critique, it should not advance.

## 4. Sequence Matters

The issue is a conversation, not a collection. Each piece must have a distinct
job inside the month.

## 5. The Reader Is Real

The target reader is not an abstraction. Hook strength, sustained interest,
recognition, pacing, and residue must all be tested explicitly.

## 6. Style Is A Constraint

Poblysh voice is calm, strategic, and intellectually serious. Generic startup
thought leadership, sales drift, and empty elegance are failures.

## 7. Facts Protect Authority

Strong ideas do not excuse weak factual discipline. Examples, references, and
risky analogies must be checked before release.

## 8. Final QA Has Veto Power

No piece is publication-ready until final QA confirms coherence, readability,
voice, and release quality.
