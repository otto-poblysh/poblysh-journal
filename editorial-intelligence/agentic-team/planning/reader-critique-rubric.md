# Reader Perspective Critique Rubric

## A. Entry Test

- Does the title create tension or curiosity?
- Does the subtitle make a concrete promise rather than a decorative gesture?
- Does the subtitle clarify who the piece is for and why they should keep
  reading?
- Does the subtitle add something the title does not already say?
- Does paragraph one earn the title?
- Do the first 2 to 4 sentences cash the subtitle's promise?
- Does the reader understand the reward for continuing?

## B. Recognition Test

- Does the reader feel this is about people like them?
- Is the startup-native texture strong enough without cliché?

## C. Movement Test

- Does the essay deepen instead of restating?
- Is there a fresh turn every 2 to 4 paragraphs?

## D. Breath Test

- Are there lines that let the mind reset?
- Is the prose too uniformly compressed?

## E. Residue Test

- Is there at least one memorable line?
- Does the ending widen thought rather than merely stop?

## F. Mobile Latazin Test

- Which lines can become statement pages?
- Which sections are too dense for phone reading?
- Where should an image-led pause occur?
