# Article Workflow

## Stage 1: Draft Brief

Owners:
- `managing_editor`
- `essay_architect`

Output:
- `essay-brief.md`

Required fields:
- working title
- thesis
- primary tension
- target reader
- desired emotional effect
- business functions touched
- what the piece must not become
- extractable lines to aim for

## Stage 2: Primary Draft

Owner:
- `lead_writer`

Goal:
- produce the first strong version of the essay

## Stage 3: Reader Critique

Owner:
- `reader_perspective_critic`

Goal:
- test title/subtitle hook, sustained interest, pacing, and mobile drift

Output:
- `critique-report.md`

Required subtitle verdict:
- pass/fail on clarity
- pass/fail on specificity of promise
- pass/fail on whether paragraph one earns it

## Stage 4: Developmental Edit

Owner:
- `developmental_editor`

Goal:
- fix structure, movement, redundancy, and transitions

## Stage 5: Style Edit

Owner:
- `style_editor`

Goal:
- restore Poblysh voice and tonal discipline

## Stage 6: Fact Integrity Review

Owner:
- `fact_checker`

Goal:
- verify claims, examples, references, and risky analogies

## Stage 7: Sequence Planning

Owner:
- `latazin_sequence_planner`

Goal:
- convert the essay into a mobile-native page rhythm

## Stage 8: Final QA

Owner:
- `final_qa`

Goal:
- approve title and subtitle strength, coherence, formatting, duplication, and
  release readiness
