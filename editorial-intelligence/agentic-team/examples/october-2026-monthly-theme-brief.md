# Monthly Theme Brief Example — October 2026

- Theme: What Gets Preserved When Attention Moves On
- Central tension: A company's strongest public moment fades faster than its
  internal meaning should allow. The question is not how to stay in the news
  but how to make the best version of the company durable enough to outlast the
  attention cycle that first made it visible.
- Why now: October sits between the late-year conference surge and year-end
  planning. Launches from September are already fading. Fundraising narratives
  begin to close or stall. The month tests whether public meaning compounds or
  simply decays once active attention is withdrawn.
- Intended reader effect: The reader should feel the fragility of attention as
  an asset and recognize that preservation is as strategic as amplification.
- Dominant pillars:
  - visibility as infrastructure
  - perception as company leverage
  - timing and attention design
- Tonal note: Quiet, almost archival. More preservation than performance. More
  long-term seriousness than seasonal urgency.
- No-go zones:
  - do not frame October as a Q4 sprint
  - do not turn preservation into content-repurposing advice
  - do not reduce the argument to "staying top of mind"
  - do not use year-end planning as a hook for checklist thinking
