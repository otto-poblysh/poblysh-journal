# Critique Report Example — June 2026 Piece 03

- Verdict: Promising thesis with strong operator relevance, but the first
  draft leaned too heavily on analogy and needed more concrete startup
  mechanics to ground the argument.
- Hook risk: Low. The opening tension between dashboard confidence and market
  opacity is immediately recognizable to operators.
- Curiosity risk: Low-to-medium. The idea lands fast, but the draft risked
  resolving too neatly without deepening into consequence.
- Pacing risk: High. The middle section carried too much analytical weight in
  sequence without a reset line. Three consecutive dense paragraphs created a
  wall that weakened mobile readability.
- Strongest lines:
  - The dashboard measures movement. The market measures meaning.
  - A company can hit every internal target and still be publicly incoherent.
- Weakest sections:
  - the overlong analogy that compared metrics to cartography without earning
    the metaphor
  - the partnership paragraph that listed examples without building toward a
    single sharpened insight
  - the closing sentence that landed too gently for a mid-issue diagnostic
    piece
- Required changes:
  - cut the cartography metaphor or reduce it to a single sentence
  - compress the partnership paragraph into a harder, shorter observation
  - add one strong statement-line to break the analytical middle
  - make the close land with more residue
- Optional improvements:
  - add a cross-domain pairing that deepens the speed-without-direction
    argument beyond startup subculture
  - sharpen one example into a subtler African operator reference
- Ready for next stage: Yes, after developmental edit
