# Critique Report Example — October 2026 Piece 04

- Verdict: The closing piece of the issue needed more structural authority. The
  first draft was reflective and well-voiced but felt more like an essay that
  happened to be last than one that gathered the month.
- Hook risk: Medium. The opening was elegant but could have been more
  immediately tense. A closing piece should begin with weight, not calm.
- Curiosity risk: Low. The thesis held well and revealed itself gradually.
- Pacing risk: Low-to-medium. The draft paced well for the first two-thirds
  but flattened in the final third, where the prose became slightly
  sentimental rather than precise.
- Strongest lines:
  - Attention is not trust. Attention is the window in which trust can be
    built.
  - The company that outlasts its own launch is the one that prepared meaning
    for the silence that follows.
- Weakest sections:
  - the third paragraph where the argument broadened without tightening
  - the reference paragraph that felt slightly forced and needed better
    integration
  - the closing two sentences that leaned toward warmth when the month
    demanded something cooler
- Required changes:
  - open with a harder first sentence that carries more tension
  - compress the broadening section and force it toward a single sharper
    consequence
  - integrate the reference so it deepens the argument rather than decorating
    it
  - rewrite the final two sentences with colder residue
- Optional improvements:
  - add a statement-line after the reference to give the piece a late pulse
  - consider whether the launch-decay metaphor can be sharpened through one
    African institutional example
- Ready for next stage: Yes, after developmental edit
