# Monthly Theme Brief Example — April 2026

- Theme: Re-entry Is A Reading Problem
- Central tension: A company can change in private and still return to public
  view under the wrong meaning.
- Why now: The month carries return, witness, mourning, resumed public life,
  and post-Q1 re-reading. That makes it a strong moment to ask how progress
  becomes publicly legible.
- Intended reader effect: The reader should feel that re-entry is not neutral
  and that public return without prepared meaning produces strategic drag.
- Dominant pillars:
  - visibility as infrastructure
  - timing and attention design
  - perception as company leverage
- Tonal note: Grave, interpretive, and calm. More witness than spectacle. More
  reading than declaring.
- No-go zones:
  - do not reduce the month to conferences or startup events
  - do not use Easter as decorative symbolism
  - do not slide into generic “be more visible” advice
  - do not make the issue feel like a productivity reset article
