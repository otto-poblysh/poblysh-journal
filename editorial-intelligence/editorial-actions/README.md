# Poblysh Journal Editorial Actions

These files describe the editorial job an essay is doing.

## Pillar alignment

Most actions should stay close to at least one of these recurring Poblysh pillars:
- visibility as infrastructure
- the cost of private traction
- legibility bias in media
- perception as leverage
- reactive teams, proactive systems

## How to choose an action

Use:
- `anatomy`, `cultural-diagnosis`, `pattern-recognition`, or `misreading-correction` when the argument depends on systems or market habits
- `close-read`, `craft-study`, `dissection`, or `comparison` when the argument depends on one artifact, phrase, or public signal
- `memory-piece`, `origin-story`, `genealogy`, or `excavation` when time and accumulation matter
- `verdict`, `portrait`, `sensory-report`, or `manifesto-piece` when the piece needs a strong authored posture
- `character-fiction` or `mythic-retelling` only when a composite scenario or rewritten cliché will genuinely sharpen the operator insight

## House rule

Even when the action is reflective, narrative, or personal, the essay must remain recognizably about startup communication, visibility, perception, timing, or public meaning.
