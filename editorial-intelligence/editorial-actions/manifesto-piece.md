# Manifesto Piece

State a Poblysh principle clearly and defend it against the strongest objections.

## Use when
- the journal needs a memorable house position
- the piece should sound authored and catalytic

## Rules
- keep the claim specific
- bring in operator consequences
- address the best counterargument

## Good fits
- visibility is infrastructure, not vanity
- startups do not have a content problem; they have a noticing problem
