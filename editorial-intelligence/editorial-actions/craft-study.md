# Craft Study

Study the craft of a narrative asset, messaging system, or public-facing artifact.

## Use when
- the power comes from how something is built
- the piece needs to show discipline, structure, or narrative design

## Focus areas
- sequence
- framing
- proof selection
- pacing
- what is withheld
- how the asset changes perception

## Good fits
- a launch note
- an investor memo
- a partnership announcement
