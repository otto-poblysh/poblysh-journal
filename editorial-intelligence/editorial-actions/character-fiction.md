# Character Fiction

Use a composite operator vignette to dramatize a tension without pretending it is literal reporting.

## Use when
- the tension is easier to feel through a scenario than a thesis
- the piece needs an emotionally legible founder, CMO, or PMM perspective

## Rules
- keep the startup setting plausible
- let the scene expose a real strategic tension
- never drift into generic fiction for its own sake

## Good fits
- a founder postponing visibility because everything feels unfinished
- a PMM carrying the narrative debt of a whole company
