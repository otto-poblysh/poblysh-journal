# Close Read

Read one line, artifact, announcement, or market phrase so closely that it opens a larger argument.

## Use when
- one sentence is doing more work than it appears to
- the publication needs to show its interpretive precision

## Questions
- What is the phrase really telling the market?
- What assumption is hidden inside it?
- What wider company reality does it reveal?

## Good fits
- "we've been heads down"
- launch headlines
- investor update phrasing
