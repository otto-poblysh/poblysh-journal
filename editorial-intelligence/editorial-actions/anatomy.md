# Anatomy

Map a system of visibility, trust, timing, or narrative debt by showing how its parts interact.

## Use when
- the reader is confused by a system rather than a single event
- the essay needs to show how incentives, teams, and signals connect

## Questions
- What are the moving parts?
- What stays hidden inside the company?
- What becomes visible outside it?

## Good fits
- how trust compounds from public signals
- how launch narratives are assembled
- how GTM teams inherit communication debt
