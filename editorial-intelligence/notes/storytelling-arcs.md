# Poblysh Journal Storytelling Arcs

Use these arcs when shaping essays.

## 1. The Hidden Cost
Best for: private traction, internal silence, unnoticed progress
- Opening: a company is moving but the market stays cold
- Turn: the cost is not operational, it is interpretive
- Close: silence is revealed as expensive

## 2. The Legibility Bias
Best for: media, fundraising headlines, market attention
- Opening: the market notices the wrong thing first
- Turn: legibility is driving the outcome
- Close: coverage is shown to reward narratable shapes

## 3. The Inherited Burden
Best for: sales, hiring, partnerships inheriting weak narrative systems
- Opening: one function is carrying too much explanation load
- Turn: that burden comes from company-wide silence
- Close: communication debt is made visible

## 4. The Quiet Signal
Best for: regulatory milestones, trust cues, institutional seriousness
- Opening: the milestone looks boring from the outside
- Turn: it quietly changes how the company is read
- Close: seriousness is reframed as a signal

## 5. The False Choice
Best for: reactive silence versus performative proactivity
- Opening: two bad modes feel like the only options
- Turn: a third path appears
- Close: the reader sees reactive proactivity as a design choice

## 6. The Timing Window
Best for: launches, partnership news, sequencing, context
- Opening: a true story arrives at the wrong moment
- Turn: timing is shown to change meaning
- Close: readiness becomes a strategic layer

## 7. The Phrase Hinge
Best for: short essays and declaration-led pieces
- Opening: one striking line
- Turn: the line opens a larger argument
- Close: the line returns in altered form and stays behind
