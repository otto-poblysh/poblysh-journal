# Principles Of Taste-Forward Poblysh Essays

## Principle 01: Tension first
Open with pressure, not a theme announcement.

## Principle 02: Operator specificity beats startup vagueness
Use scenes a founder, CMO, PMM, or GTM lead would actually recognize.

## Principle 03: Communication is a strategic layer
Treat visibility, timing, and perception as business infrastructure.

## Principle 04: Calm is part of the argument
The sentence should never sound more eager than the thought requires.

## Principle 05: Product stays offstage
The worldview should lead. The product can remain implied unless the essay explicitly needs it.

## Principle 06: Legibility matters
Keep asking what the outside world can read from the inside motion.

## Principle 07: Timing is not a tactic
Treat timing as an interpretive discipline, not a posting schedule.

## Principle 08: Use judgment
Poblysh should sound authored. It should not hide behind frameworks when a clean sentence will do more.

## Principle 09: Residue beats recap
Close with a line that stays with the reader, not a summary paragraph that empties the thought.

## Principle 10: No generic startup swagger
Avoid overclaiming, hype language, and content-operator clichés.
