# Founder Objection Map

Use this note when planning monthly issues or briefing individual essays.

Poblysh gets stronger when it treats founder resistance as rational rather than
ignorant. The point is not to tell experienced operators that press matters. It
is to show why they still resist investing attention in it, then make the
Poblysh system answer feel more believable than the objection.

## 1. No time because product and sales come first

Private sentence:
- "I know visibility matters, but I cannot justify taking time away from product
  development, customers, or revenue."

Why it is rational:
- early teams are small
- founder attention is already overloaded
- comms work feels secondary until a high-stakes moment arrives

Poblysh answer:
- do not turn founders into part-time PR managers
- use one-time setup and ambient signal capture
- let the system discover and preserve story candidates before they go cold

Good essay directions:
- visibility has been left too manual to sustain
- narrative systems protect product and sales time
- reactivity is not the problem; missing infrastructure is

## 2. No PR skill or media instinct

Private sentence:
- "Even if we wanted to do this, we do not know what counts as a story or how
  to pitch it."

Why it is rational:
- most founders were not trained in media framing
- story selection is a specialist skill
- bad pitches create reputational drag

Poblysh answer:
- the system supplies the discovery, framing, and drafting skill
- founders approve, enrich, or decline rather than invent from scratch
- niche journalist matching reduces guesswork

Good essay directions:
- story discovery is a skill layer, not a personality trait
- AI can lower the cost of editorial noticing
- founder judgement should approve the story, not manufacture it alone

## 3. No money for a PR agency

Private sentence:
- "This is all fine for better-funded startups, but we cannot pay agency
  retainers."

Why it is rational:
- agencies are expensive
- early-stage teams optimize scarce cash against product, hiring, and growth
- agency access has historically bundled skill, process, and relationships

Poblysh answer:
- unbundle the agency into workflows founders can afford
- draft the pitch, match the journalist, and send directly
- preserve the advantage of founder voice without requiring founder chaos

Good essay directions:
- the system replaces agency dependency with structured capability
- founder voice can be an advantage when the pitch is well-shaped
- good infrastructure is cheaper than retained improvisation

## 4. Press only cares about funding or very large milestones

Private sentence:
- "Journalists ignore anything that is not a raise, a huge launch, or a dramatic
  milestone."

Why it is rational:
- funding is legible and easy to narrate
- discovery is expensive for journalists
- many real company signals look boring until interpreted well

Poblysh answer:
- not every update should be pitched
- boring stories can still accumulate in the newsroom as public proof
- the system should only pitch when there is real audience relevance

Good essay directions:
- easy-to-cover news dominates because discovery costs are real
- small signals matter when they change how a company is read
- public record can compound even when nothing is pitched

## 5. Fear of sounding self-important, salesy, or fraudulent

Private sentence:
- "I do not want to perform progress or sound like I am overselling unfinished
  work."

Why it is rational:
- many founders already feel impostor syndrome
- startup media behavior often rewards overstatement
- self-promotion can feel emotionally misaligned with the work

Poblysh answer:
- do not pitch everything
- do not inflate weak stories
- let unpitched updates live in the newsroom as searchable proof
- only share what has audience consequence

Good essay directions:
- restraint is part of the system, not a personal weakness
- seriousness looks selective, not loud
- the answer to fraud-feeling is better filters, not more performance

## 6. Distrust of generic "founders should post more" advice

Private sentence:
- "I am tired of advice that reduces serious company building to content
  cadence."

Why it is rational:
- most visibility advice ignores the actual labor of building
- it flattens experienced operators into beginners
- it confuses output volume with market legibility

Poblysh answer:
- do not moralize consistency
- interpret visibility as infrastructure, timing, and signal preservation
- keep the company, not the content calendar, at the center

Good essay directions:
- the real problem is not effort, but system design
- visibility is a trust layer, not a posting habit
- market legibility comes from preserved meaning, not volume

## 7. Planning Use

When planning a month:
- choose 1 or 2 objections for the issue to carry
- assign one primary objection to each essay
- vary the answers so the issue does not repeat the same reassurance four times

When briefing an essay:
- name the objection explicitly
- let the opening acknowledge it early
- make the system answer visible through scenes, not only thesis language

When reviewing a draft:
- ask whether the reader could still say "I know, but I have my reasons"
- if yes, the objection has not been handled strongly enough
