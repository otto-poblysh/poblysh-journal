# Do Not Reference

Avoid grounding essays in:
- generic content-marketing best practices
- shallow "founder brand" advice
- vanity-metric discussions with no strategic frame
- empty "thought leadership" language
- clichés about shipping faster unless the piece is genuinely about timing
- references that make Poblysh sound like a PR agency blog

Also avoid:
- legacy publication framing unrelated to startup communication
- culture-specific references that do not serve the startup communication argument
