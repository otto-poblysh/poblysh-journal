## Page Strategy Summary

This 25-page sequence is designed as a high-density editorial experience that mirrors the "compounding cost" theme of the essay. It avoids the "slide deck" feel by maintaining a strong prose floor (2-3 paragraphs per reading page) while using monochrome color blocks to represent the "weight" of the explanation burden.

### Background Rhythm Distribution
- **Light Monochrome (Paper/Bone/Ash)**: 8 pages (32%). Used for calm reading, reflective examples, and the 'Plaid' success case.
- **Mid Monochrome (Slate/Iron)**: 9 pages (36%). Used for technical analogies, operational drag analysis, and the 'suspect vs trust' mechanics.
- **Dark Monochrome (Charcoal/True Black)**: 8 pages (32%). Used for the opening case study, the "Narrative Debt" reveal, the "Invoice" hinge, and the final shared liability conclusion.

**Rhythm Pattern**: D-D-M-L-M-D-M-L-M-L-L-M-M-L-M-D-D-M-L-M-L-L-M-D-D
*No more than 2 consecutive light pages. No more than 3 consecutive pages of any ground.*

## Extraction and Editing Notes
1. **The Hook**: Extracted the "West African payments" story into a 5-page opening arc to build the frustration of the "22 minutes of context".
2. **The Analogy**: Grouped the technical debt comparison into a 2-page logic block (Pages 6-7).
3. **The Redistribution**: Focused on the "Mechanism" (Page 11) as a midpoint transition from theory to operational impact.
4. **The Comparison**: Placed the Plaid success story as the "Light/Calm" contrast to the "Mid/Stress" of the debt narrative.
5. **The Hinge**: Used the concept of "The Invoice" (Page 17) as the story's peak tension point before the final distribution logic.

## Closing Note
The sequence works because it uses the "Weight" of the monochrome grounds to simulate the "debt". The movement from the dark, heavy intro into the light, calm Plaid story, and back into the dark "Invoice" hinge creates a physical sense of the burden being redistributed. Every page carries enough prose (2-3 paragraphs) to satisfy a high-intellect reader while using visual pauses to emphasize the structural nature of "Narrative Debt".
